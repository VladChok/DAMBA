# Research notes — BERING / 1990

## Historical anchors

- Late 19th / early 20th centuries: proposals for linking the continents by rail were floated, but no intercontinental rail tunnel was built.
- 1950s: Soviet engineer Pyotr Borisov promoted a Bering Strait pumping dam concept intended to alter Arctic water circulation. It was not an approved construction project or an established climate solution.
- **1 June 1990:** U.S.–Soviet maritime boundary agreement and joint statement supporting a Soviet–U.S. international park around the Bering Strait. None constitutes a construction contract for a dam, railway, or tunnel.
- **1991:** The Soviet Union dissolved.

- U.S.–Soviet international park joint statement (1 June 1990): https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/soviet-united-states-joint-statement-the-establishment-soviet-united-states-international
- Maritime boundary statement (1 June 1990): https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/statement-the-united-states-soviet-maritime-boundary-agreement

## Counterfactual branch

Selected divergence: **1 June 1990**, additional hypothetical diplomatic agreement to commission feasibility studies for a Bering intercontinental crossing. This is not proof that either party seriously considered such a deal in those talks.

Potential partnerships with American banks, Japan, and Europe are plausible subjects of negotiation, not predetermined outcomes. Loan financing creates liabilities; project contracts do not automatically change national institutions or guarantee democratic, financial, or political outcomes.

## Money

Historic money actually spent on Bering dam **construction: no construction took place**. There is no documented, agreed 1990 dam-construction budget in this study.

The $120B displayed on the website is a **scenario assumption**, not an archival number, validated civil-engineering estimate, or historical cost. Its split is illustrative only: rail 65B; crossing 25B; energy 12B; ports and logistics 8B; study 4B; contingency 6B. All in hypothetical circa-1990 USD. Full climate pumping dam costs are excluded.

## Limits

- The political-opportunity ranking is an opinion, not a quantitative historical dataset.
- The scenario curves are symbolic, not GDP, trade, or welfare forecasts.
- Do not conflate sea barriers, climate pumps, railway bridges, and subsea tunnels.
- Do not claim that the Soviet collapse would be averted by a project, that Japan would resolve territorial disputes, or that Western financial institutions would become major Russian lenders automatically.
- Need independent expert review before any numerical environmental impact claims.
